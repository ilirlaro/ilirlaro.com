Blog

Writing, observations and things I’m still working out.

A place for ideas that sit around the edges of my day job: crypto distribution, product and growth, markets, technology, and the occasional question that has nothing to do with any of them.

Why write

Most of my professional writing has historically been created for a company, a launch or a specific business objective. I wanted somewhere to think in public without every idea needing to become a presentation, campaign or internal document. Some of these pieces come directly from problems I have worked on; others are simply subjects I keep returning to and want to understand more clearly.

I do not expect every view here to remain unchanged. Part of the point is to write down how I see something today, expose the assumptions underneath it and leave enough room to change my mind when better information appears.

Why crypto products still have a distribution problem

Great technology does not remove the need to earn attention, trust and repeated customer behaviour. In crypto, distribution is often the harder product problem.
